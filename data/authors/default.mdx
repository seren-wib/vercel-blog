Hi, I'm **seren-wib**, a third-year student in the School of Software at Chungbuk National University who learns by
building and writing things down.
Two things interest me most: handing repetitive work to AI agents and scripts, and turning stories I imagine into games.
Beyond that, I build web apps end to end in small teams and keep them honest with CI and pull-request rules. Lecture notes end up here as structured posts.

## Interests

- **AI automation**: I keep the config, hooks and memory of three agents (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini) in one repo shared across Windows and macOS, and built a USB kit that handles school lab PC logins in one command (usb-kit). I also designed, and have long been using, a workflow where the agent takes work from issue to merge through per-step skills (Atlas Odyssey)
- **Game development**: I'm prototyping Vane, a 2D story adventure in Unity, and published a Victoria 3 mod. My Unity study notes live in the Unity category of this blog
- **Full-stack web**: React front ends on top of Express or Django back ends, with JWT auth and relational databases
- **CI and team workflow**: GitHub Actions on every pull request, protected branches, issue- and PR-based collaboration
- **Computer science fundamentals**: course notes written up chapter by chapter on this blog
